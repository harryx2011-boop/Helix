# 0004. UI revamp takes Firecrawl's patterns, not its look

Status: Accepted (Harry, 2026-11-11; `docs/plans/2026-11-11-helix-v3-revamp.md`, "Decisions (Harry, 2026-11-11)")

## Context

Harry asked for a UI revamp of all four surfaces (marketing site, dashboard, playground, docs), with likeness to Firecrawl's site as a reference.

## Decision

- Patterns from Firecrawl, with Helix's own identity.
- No Firecrawl colours, marks, art or copy.

## Consequences

- Phase 6 (UI revamp) designs its own palette, marks and copy.
- Reference studies (the audit files in `docs/audits/2026-11-11-firecrawl-*`) inform patterns only.
